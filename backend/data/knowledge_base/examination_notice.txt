Category: Examination
Examination Form Submission

Students must submit the semester examination registration form through the student portal by 15 October. A late submission window remains open until 20 October with a late fee. Verify the final date on the latest examination office circular before payment. Admit cards are issued after the form is verified.

Semester examinations begin on 4 December. The detailed examination timetable is published on the examination notice board and student portal.
